===============================================================================
 KANAGAWA DRAGON (ON DEEP CHARCOAL) COLOR PALETTE
===============================================================================

Description:
  An earthy, muted Japanese aesthetic adapted from Kanagawa Dragon, paired
  with a deep matte charcoal background (#141312) for enhanced focus and
  ultra-low eye strain. Features warm amber, moss green, sumi inks, and terracotta accents.
